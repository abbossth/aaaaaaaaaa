# 18-dars. Pseudo-class (hover/focus), pseudo-element, position (sticky/fixed)

**Guruh:** 8.10 | **Turi:** 📘 Mavzu | **Davomiyligi:** 80 daqiqa

## Maqsad
- Pseudo-class'larni ishlatadi: `:hover`, `:focus`, `:active`, `:first-child`, `:nth-child()`, `:checked`.
- Pseudo-element'larni ishlatadi: `::before`, `::after` (content bilan), `::placeholder`, `::selection`.
- `position` qiymatlarini farqlaydi: `static`, `relative`, `absolute`, `fixed`, `sticky` va `top/right/bottom/left`, `z-index`.
- Sticky menyu, "yuqoriga" tugmasi (fixed), kartochkadagi yorliq (absolute) yasaydi.
