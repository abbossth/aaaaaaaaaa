# 30-dars mentor eslatmasi

- Blok-sxema (Flowgorithm, 5-dars) va JS kodni doskada yonma-yon chizing: romb = `if`, "Ha" = `{...}`, "Yo'q" = `else {...}`.
- Tipik xatolar: `if (x = 5)`, `else if` o'rniga ketma-ket `if`lar, `prompt` natijasini `Number()` siz solishtirish (`"9" > "10"` → true!).
