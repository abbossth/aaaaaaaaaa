# 30-dars amaliy topshiriq

## 🟢 Oson (+5 XP)
1. **Juft yoki toq:** son so'rab, `alert` bilan javob (`% 2 === 0`).
2. **Musbat/manfiy/nol.**
3. **Parol:** parol so'rang. `"12345"` bo'lsa "Xush kelibsiz!", aks holda "Xato parol!".

## 🟡 O'rta (+10 XP)
4. **Baho aniqlagich:** 0–100 ball → baho (5-darsdagi Flowgorithm vazifasi, endi JS'da!). 100 dan katta yoki manfiy bo'lsa "Noto'g'ri ball".
5. **Kinoteatr:** yosh so'raladi. 7 gacha bepul, 7–17: 20 000, 18+: 35 000 so'm.

## 🔴 Qiyin (+20 XP)
6. **Hafta kuni (switch):** 1–7 raqam → kun nomi (1 → Dushanba).
7. **Kalkulyator:** ikki son va amal (`+ - * /`) so'rab natija. 0 ga bo'lishni tekshiring!
