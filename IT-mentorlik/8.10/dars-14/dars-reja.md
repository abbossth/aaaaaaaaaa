# 14-dars. Background, border, box model (margin/padding)

**Guruh:** 8.10 | **Turi:** 📘 Mavzu | **Davomiyligi:** 80 daqiqa

## Maqsad
- Fon xossalarini ishlatadi: `background-color`, `background-image`, `background-size: cover`, `background-position`, gradient (`linear-gradient`).
- Chegara xossalarini ishlatadi: `border` (qalinlik, stil, rang), `border-radius`, `box-shadow`.
- **Box model**ni tushunadi: content → padding → border → margin. `width`, `height`, `box-sizing: border-box`.
- DevTools'da box model'ni ko'ra oladi.
- Chiroyli "profil kartochkasi" yasaydi.
