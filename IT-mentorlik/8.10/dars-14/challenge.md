# 14-dars challenge: "Kartochka jangi" ⚔️

**Vaqt:** 15 daqiqa | **Format:** individual

**Mavzu:** "Sevimli futbolchi (yoki qahramon) kartochkasi" — FIFA kartochkalari uslubida!

## Talablar
- Rasm, ism, jamoa, 3 ta xususiyat (masalan, tezlik 95, zarba 90, pas 88)
- Gradient fon yoki `background-image`
- `border-radius`, `box-shadow`
- Kamida 3 xil `padding`/`margin` qiymati

## Baholash
Barcha kartochkalar ekranda ko'rsatiladi, sinf ovoz beradi (har kim 2 ta ovoz, o'ziga bermaydi).
