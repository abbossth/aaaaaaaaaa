# 3-dars challenge: "Chek printer" 🧾

**Vaqt:** 15 daqiqa | **Format:** juftlik

Do'kon uchun terminal chek dasturini yozing. Dastur 3 ta mahsulot nomi, narxi va sonini so'raydi va quyidagiga o'xshash chek chiqaradi:

```
==============================
        "IT MARKET" DO'KONI
==============================
Mahsulot        Soni      Summa
------------------------------
Non                2      8,000
Sut                1     12,500
Shokolad           3     45,000
------------------------------
JAMI:                    65,500
QQS (12%):                7,860
==============================
   Xaridingiz uchun rahmat!
==============================
```

## Talablar
- Ustunlar f-string bilan tekislangan (`:<15`, `:>5`, `:>10,`)
- QQS 12% alohida hisoblangan
- **Bonus +10 XP:** 100 000 so'mdan ortiq xaridga 5% chegirma qatori chiqsin (hali `if` o'tilmagan, shuning uchun o'zingiz topib ishlating!)

## Baholash
To'g'ri hisob + chiroyli format. Mentor 3 ta eng yaxshisini tanlaydi: +30/+20/+10.
Namuna: `kod/chek.py`
