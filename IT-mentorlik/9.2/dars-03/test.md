# 3-dars tezkor test

1. `type(3.0)`? — `float`
2. `input()` qaytaradigan tur? — `str`
3. `int("12") + 3`? — `15`
4. `"12" + "3"`? — `"123"`
5. `f"{3.14159:.2f}"`? — `"3.14"`
6. Qaysi nom to'g'ri: `2ism`, `ism-2`, `ism_2`? — `ism_2`
7. `int("abc")` qanday xato beradi? — `ValueError`
8. `bool(0)` va `bool("0")`? — `False` va `True` (bo'sh bo'lmagan satr)
