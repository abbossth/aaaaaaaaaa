# 22-dars. Agile, Scrum, Kanban

**Guruh:** 11.1 | **Turi:** 📘 Mavzu | **Davomiyligi:** 80 daqiqa
**Qo'llanma:** IV bob, "Agile, Scrum, Kanban metodologiyasi" (sprintlar uchun oldinga ko'chirilgan)

## Maqsad
- Agile manifestining 4 qadriyati va asosiy g'oyasini tushunadi.
- Scrum: rollar (Product Owner, Scrum Master, Developers), artefaktlar (Product Backlog, Sprint Backlog, Increment, DoD) va tadbirlar (Planning, Daily, Review, Retrospective).
- Kanban: vizual oqim, WIP limit, lead time / cycle time, "bottleneck" (tor joy).
- Scrum va Kanban'ni taqqoslab, jamoasi uchun "Scrumban" yondashuvini tanlaydi.
- Jamoaning Scrum rollari va sprint jadvalini belgilaydi.
