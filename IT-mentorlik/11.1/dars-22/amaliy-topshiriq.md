# 22-dars amaliy topshiriq: jamoa Scrum sozlamalari (+20 XP)

`docs/scrum.md` faylini yarating (`kod/scrum-shablon.md`):

1. **Rollar:** Product Owner, Scrum Master (tech lead bo'lishi shart emas!), Developers.
2. **Sprint ritmi:** Sprint 1 = 25–26-darslar (2 hafta). Sprint Planning — 23-dars, Review + Retro — 26-dars oxirida.
3. **Daily Scrum:** har dars boshida 5 daqiqa (3 savol).
4. **Definition of Done:** kamida 5 punkt.
5. **Kanban board ustunlari va WIP limitlari.**
6. **Kommunikatsiya:** Telegram guruh qoidalari (javob berish vaqti, texnik savollar formati — 20-dars).
