# 30-dars uyga vazifa

## Majburiy (+10 XP)
Bandit'da kamida 10-darajaga yeting.

## Qo'shimcha
Har 5 ta yangi daraja uchun +5 XP (yil davomida). 20+ daraja — maxsus sovg'a 🎁

## Keyingi darsga
Keyingi darsdan — **Docker** 🐳! Iloji bo'lsa, **Docker Desktop** o'rnating (docker.com). Muqobil: **labs.play-with-docker.com** (brauzerda, Docker Hub akkaunti bilan).
