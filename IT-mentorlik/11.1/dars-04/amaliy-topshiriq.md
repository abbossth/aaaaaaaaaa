# 4-dars amaliy topshiriq (jamoaviy)

## 🟢 Oson (+5 XP)
1. Jamoa reposida **Issues** yoqilganini tekshiring. Label'larni sozlang: `bug`, `feature`, `docs`, `design`, `priority: high`.
2. `.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/bug_report.md` shablonini qo'shing (`kod/bug_report.md` asosida).

## 🟡 O'rta (+10 XP)
3. **GitHub Projects** (repo → Projects → New project → Board) yarating. Ustunlar: Backlog, To Do, In Progress, Review, Done.
4. Startap g'oyasi hali tanlanmagan, shuning uchun **jamoa landing sahifasi** uchun 6 ta issue yarating: header, "biz haqimizda", jamoa kartochkalari, footer, responsiv, deploy. Har biriga assignee va label qo'ying.

## 🔴 Qiyin (+20 XP)
5. Har bir a'zo o'ziga tayinlangan bitta issue'ni bajarib, commit xabarida `Closes #N` yozib push qilsin. Issue avtomatik yopildimi va board'da "Done"ga o'tdimi? (Board'ning Workflows sozlamasida "Item closed → Done" yoqilgan bo'lishi kerak.)
6. Ikki kishi bir vaqtda `README.md` ning **bir xil qatorini** o'zgartirib push qilsin. Nima bo'ldi? (Bu keyingi 2 darsning "spoiler"i.)
