# 5-dars uyga vazifa

## Majburiy (+10 XP)
Landing page'ni "Qiyin" darajagacha tugating (responsiv). Figma faylingiz havolasini (Share → "Anyone with the link can view") va sahifa skrinshotini yuboring.

## Qo'shimcha (+10 XP)
**Figma Community**'dan bepul landing page maketini toping va uning bitta bo'limini (masalan, pricing yoki testimonials) kodga aylantiring.
