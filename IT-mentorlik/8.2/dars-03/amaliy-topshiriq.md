# 3-dars amaliy topshiriq: "Mening sahifam"ga dizayn

2-darsda yaratgan sahifangizga `style.css` faylini ulang: `<link rel="stylesheet" href="style.css">`.

## 🟢 Oson (+5 XP)
- `body`: shrift (`font-family`), fon rangi, `margin: 0`
- `header`: fon rangi, `padding`, oq matn
- `nav` havolalari: pastki chiziqsiz (`text-decoration: none`), hover'da rang o'zgaradi

## 🟡 O'rta (+10 XP)
- `header` Flexbox bilan: logo chapda, menyu o'ngda (`justify-content: space-between`)
- `#sevimli` bo'limidagi 3 ta `article` Grid bilan bir qatorda: `repeat(3, 1fr)`
- Kartochkalarga `border-radius`, `box-shadow`, `padding`

## 🔴 Qiyin (+20 XP)
- Forma chiroyli bo'lsin: label'lar yuqorida, input'lar to'liq kenglikda (`display: flex; flex-direction: column`)
- Kartochkalar 600px'dan kichik ekranda bittadan ustunda tursin (`@media`). *Bu keyingi dars mavzusi, lekin o'zingiz urinib ko'ring!*
